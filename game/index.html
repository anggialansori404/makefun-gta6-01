VELVET TIDE

An open-world crime story in a city that never dries off.

Nico Ferreira washes up in Port Azura with a duffel bag and a debt. Sol Vega, who runs the pawn shop on 4th & Coral, has work: crates, cars, cold vans and one last score under the Gull Point light. Drive anything. Run from the PAPD. Get rich or get busted trying.

WASD / Arrows move & drive · E enter / exit car · Shift sprint · Space handbrake · H horn
Mouse aim · Click fire · P / Esc pause · Follow the gold marker, mind the stars.
